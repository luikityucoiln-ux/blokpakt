Back up your planner

Download a copy of your entries and settings to keep for later or move to another device. To bring them back, choose Restore backup and select the file you saved. You do not need to open or edit the file.

Restoring replaces your current entries and settings; it does not combine them. Download a backup first if you want to keep your current data. Backups are not encrypted, so store them privately. Website and downloaded copies do not sync.

Download offline planner

The offline planner download is a fresh tool with examples, not a copy of your figures. Download a backup too, then open the offline planner and restore it. Back up before moving the planner, changing browsers, or clearing browser data. Some browsers limit saving local files; keep a backup and check the save status.

No accounts, trackers, bank connections, or automatic network requests. Website links and downloads go online only when you choose them. Local storage is not an encrypted vault or an access-controlled account. This is a planning estimate, not financial advice or a service quote.
