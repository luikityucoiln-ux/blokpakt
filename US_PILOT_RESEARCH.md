## Evidence Needed Before Shortlisting

Collect five verified businesses per city. Count distinct operating businesses,
not multiple pages for one business; distinguish franchises from independents.
For each business record:

- Official business name, exact source URL, and date checked.
- Explicitly stated service area and driveway cleaning service.
- Public price, if any, including units, minimum charge, scope, exclusions, and
  whether it is a starting price. Otherwise record "not publicly verified".
- Official email or inquiry-form URL, including whether the form requires a call,
  full address, or other information before it can be used.
- Whether a neighborhood offer is explicitly published. Never infer willingness
  to discount or partner from an ordinary quote form.
- Unknown licensing, insurance, availability, and subcontracting requirements.
  Marketing claims are not independent verification.

Prefer the city with a tractable small service area and verified written contact
routes, not an unsupported score. Only recommend outreach after source review;
provider replies are still needed to verify pricing and operational feasibility.
