blokpakt / Home & time planner

A new home for your planner.

The standalone tool is now in public/home-planner.html so it can also be downloaded from the website. This small page keeps the old file location available for recovering your existing local data.

  1. Download a backup below in the browser where you used the old planner.
  2. Open the new planner, choose Backup & data, then Restore backup and select the file you saved. You do not need to open or edit it.
  3. Check your entries and settings before removing an old copy.

Nothing is uploaded, deleted, or automatically replaced. Local-file storage varies by browser, and the website does not share data with a downloaded copy. Backups contain your figures and are not encrypted.
